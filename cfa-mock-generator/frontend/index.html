About MockGen

What is MockGen?

MockGen generates Level I format mock exams from your study PDFs. AI creates 3-choice MCQs aligned with official learning outcomes. Exams are two 90-question sessions with a break.

All data stays local. Only text chunks go to Cohere AI.
